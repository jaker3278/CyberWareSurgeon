WHAT HAPPENS HERE

YOU BRING THE PROBLEM.
WE MODIFY REALITY.

CyberWareSurgeon exists for hardware that cannot simply be bought from a shelf.

Wearable electronics. Custom interfaces. Strange enclosures. Embedded systems. Modified machines. Experimental objects.

The aim is not to make technology look futuristic.

The aim is to make technology do things it was never originally designed to do.
