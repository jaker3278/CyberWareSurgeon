OPERATION BRIEF

WEARABLE HARDWARE AS A DESIGNED OBJECT.

Biluxi Watch explores the watch as more than a timekeeping device: a compact piece of wearable technology where interface, casing and visual identity operate as one system.

The project focuses on product form, surface treatment, interface presentation and the physical relationship between technology and the body.

Within the CyberWareSurgeon archive, it sits naturally beside IZM Glasses as another exploration of wearable hardware designed with a strong identity.
