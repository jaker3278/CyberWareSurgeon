LUMA POCKET CORE

CWS-P001 ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
VISUAL RECORD LUMA POCKET CORE // PROJECT 001
01 / 08

OPERATION BRIEF

A SMALL CORE FOR A BIGGER CONNECTION.

LUMA Pocket Core explores the idea of carrying a persistent digital companion as a physical, tactile object rather than something trapped inside a conventional phone interface.

The project combines a circular display, custom software, a protective 3D-printable enclosure and a deliberately visible, mechanical cyberware aesthetic.

IZM GLASSES

CWS-P002 CLIENT DESIGN
VISUAL RECORD IZM GLASSES // PROJECT 002
01 / 08

OPERATION BRIEF

WEARABLE TECHNOLOGY DESIGNED AS IDENTITY.

IZM Glasses was created for Biluxi Designs as a headset concept intended to extend the visual language of the client's existing projects and strengthen the identity of the wider brand.

The design process moved through exploratory forms and visual studies before resolving into a final client design.

For CyberWareSurgeon, the project sits directly at the intersection of industrial design, wearable hardware and speculative cyberware.

PROJECT // 003

BILUXI WATCH

CWS-P003 WEARABLE DESIGN
VISUAL RECORD BILUXI WATCH // PROJECT 003
01 / 08

OPERATION BRIEF

WEARABLE HARDWARE AS A DESIGNED OBJECT.

Biluxi Watch explores the watch as more than a timekeeping device: a compact piece of wearable technology where interface, casing and visual identity operate as one system.

The project focuses on product form, surface treatment, interface presentation and the physical relationship between technology and the body.

Within the CyberWareSurgeon archive, it sits naturally beside IZM Glasses as another exploration of wearable hardware designed with a strong identity.
