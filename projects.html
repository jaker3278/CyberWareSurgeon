LUMA POCKET CORE

CWS-P001 ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

OPERATION BRIEF

A SMALL CORE FOR A BIGGER CONNECTION.

LUMA Pocket Core explores the idea of carrying a persistent digital companion as a physical, tactile object rather than something trapped inside a conventional phone interface.

The project combines a circular display, custom software, a protective 3D-printable enclosure and a deliberately visible, mechanical cyberware aesthetic.
